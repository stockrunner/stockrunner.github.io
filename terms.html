What the app is

Clip Uploader ("the App") is a private desktop tool run by its owner on their own computer. It uploads short videos the owner has edited to the owner's own TikTok account, where they arrive as drafts in the TikTok inbox. Nothing is posted publicly by the App; the owner reviews and publishes each draft in the TikTok app.

Who may use it

The App is not offered to the public. Only the owner's TikTok account is connected to it.

Content

  • The owner is responsible for having the rights to every video the App uploads, and for following TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • The App does not change, sell or share any content outside the upload to the owner's own account.

Stopping use

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. Deleting the App from the computer removes its saved sign-in.
