Clip Uploader

A personal desktop app that sends my own edited gameplay clips to my own TikTok account as drafts.

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

What the App accesses

  • Basic TikTok profile information needed to sign in (your TikTok user ID and display name).
  • Permission to upload videos to your account as drafts (the video.upload scope).

How it is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, encrypted with the operating system's per-user protection. It is never sent anywhere except to TikTok's own servers when uploading.

What the App does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No sharing or selling of any data to third parties.
  • No access to messages, followers, comments or other accounts.

Deleting your data

Withdraw access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions) and delete the App's saved sign-in file from the computer. No other copy exists.
